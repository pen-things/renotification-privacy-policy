Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

ReNotification! is designed to help users manage notification reminders and repetition. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the app handles information and permissions.

1. Information and Data

ReNotification! does not operate its own server to collect or store your personal information. App data is processed locally on your device to provide its features.

2. Notification Access

The app requires Notification Access permission to detect and manage notifications according to your settings. Notification content may be processed locally to provide reminder, repetition, filtering, and history features.

3. Data Storage

Settings, reminder information, and notification history may be stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Permissions

ReNotification! may request permissions or system access necessary for features you choose to use, such as notification access, alarms, sound, and background operation. These permissions are used only to support the app's functionality.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

ReNotification! does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers. The app does not use its own advertising or analytics tracking services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage or delete notification history through the app's available controls. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to Android system behavior and any backups you independently control.

7. Security

Data is stored on your device. We recommend keeping your device protected with appropriate screen-lock and security settings.

8. Children's Privacy

ReNotification! is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
